Section 1 • Problem Context

Quantified Consumer & Environmental Pain Points

Evaluating the hidden economic, acoustic, and physiological costs of standard household RO water purifiers in India.

1 Brine Wastage & Environmental Depletion

For every 1 liter of pure drinking water produced, standard residential RO systems discharge up to 3 liters of high-salinity brine waste into sewers. In urban Indian households, this wastes over 120 liters of tap water per household per day.

2 Physiological Mineral Depletion (WHO 2005)

Fixed RO membranes strip divalent cations (Ca²⁺, Mg²⁺), reducing TDS below 50 mg/L. Chronic consumption increases diuresis by 20% and leaches vital bone minerals, violating WHO and BIS drinking standards.

3 High Maintenance Friction & Noise

Piston booster pumps generate harsh 62 dB acoustic noise and rely on disposable polypropylene pre-filters requiring frequent manual service visits costing ₹2,000+ per year.

Section 2 • Engineering Approach

Our Solution Architecture (Bento Grid)

Combining multi-parameter sensing, closed-loop fluid routing, and tool-free DIY servicing into a cohesive home purifications system.

⚙️

Closed-Loop Proportional Blending

An ESP32-controlled servo 3-way valve dynamically splits feed water between a 100 GPD RO membrane and a 0.01μm UF bypass, maintaining target TDS (150–250 mg/L) without artificial chemical re-mineralization cartridges.

Dynamic Mass Balance α = 0.0 to 1.0
🔬

Multi-Sensor Telemetry Array

Integrates dual piezoresistive differential pressure (ΔP) transducers, 860nm optical turbidity sensor, bipolar electrical conductivity (TDS), and pH sensing for continuous water quality monitoring.

Real-Time Edge Analytics 100 Hz Sampling
🛡️

Auto-Flush & Tool-Free DIY Swaps

A stainless steel 40μm mesh spin-down filter automatically flushes sediment every 24 hours. Twist-lock bayonet manifolds allow 30-second filter replacements with zero tools required.

Zero Technician Dependency < 30s Replacement
